For rights holders: notices and takedown

Version dated 17 November 2026.

We respect the rights of others and respond to substantiated complaints. If you believe that material on ms-dos.su or in the MBFUTool program infringes your rights, write to us — we will look into it and, if necessary, remove it.

1. What is hosted on the site

The site is informational: articles, documentation, download pages. There are no user uploads, comments or publication of third-party files here. The MBFUTool program does not contain MS-DOS, Norton Commander or other proprietary product files — see the “Third-party licences” section for details.

2. Where to send a notice

Email: {{EMAIL_LEGAL}} (subject line — “Copyright”). The postal address is in the “Contacts and seller details” section.

3. What to include in the notice (DMCA, 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3))

  1. a physical or electronic signature of the rights holder or their representative;
  2. a description of the work whose rights are infringed;
  3. the exact URLs of the pages or files you ask to be removed;
  4. your name, address, telephone number and e-mail;
  5. a statement of good-faith belief that the use is not authorised by the rights holder, the law or a licence;
  6. a statement, under liability for false information, that the information is accurate and that you are acting on behalf of the rights holder;
  7. consent to the jurisdiction of the court at the defendant's location (for the USA) and to acceptance of service of process.

4. What happens next

  • we confirm receipt within 5 working days;
  • we review the complaint and, if it is substantiated, remove or restrict access to the material;
  • we inform the author of the material and the complainant of the decision;
  • in disputed cases we give an opportunity to submit objections.

5. Counter-notice (USA)

If your material has been removed and you believe the removal was mistaken, send a counter-notice to the address above. It must contain your signature, a description of the material and its location before removal, a statement under liability of a good-faith belief in a mistake, your contact details and consent to the jurisdiction of a federal court. We may restore the material after 10–14 working days if the rights holder does not file a claim.

6. Russian law

We act as an information intermediary within the meaning of art. 1253.1 of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation: we do not modify the material, are not aware of its unlawfulness before receiving a notice and eliminate the infringement after it. Claims are accepted in writing at the address in the “Contacts” section; the review period is 10 working days.

7. Liability for false notices

A notice containing knowingly false information may entail liability, including compensation for damages (17 U.S.C. § 512(f), art. 306 of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation). Please verify the facts before sending.

8. Repeat infringement

If a user systematically infringes rights, we terminate the provision of access to the project's materials to that user.
