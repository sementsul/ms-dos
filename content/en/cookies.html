Cookies and Analytics

Revision of 17 November 2026.

The ms-dos.su website runs without analytical cookies until you click “Accept analytics”. Refusal does not restrict anything — all pages and files are available.

1. What we enable and when

  • Necessary. The site has no necessary cookies of its own. Your choice is stored in the browser's localStorage rather than in a cookie, and is not sent to the server.
  • Analytical. Yandex.Metrica and Google Analytics. They are enabled only after explicit consent. They record anonymised statistics: which pages are opened, where visitors come from, how long they read.
  • Advertising. There are none: we do not set advertising pixels, do not use retargeting and do not collect cross-site profiles.

3. What exactly is stored in localStorage

The key mbfu_consent with a value like:

{"v":"1","analytics":true,"ts":"2026-11-17T20:00:00.000Z"}

This is your choice (“analytics allowed / disallowed”), the version of the consent and the time of the click. The data stays in the browser and is not transferred to the server.

4. How to change or withdraw consent

  • the “Cookie settings” button in the footer of any page;
  • clearing the site data in the browser settings — then the banner will appear again;
  • an email to {{EMAIL_LEGAL}} if you need the related data on the analytics side to be deleted.

On withdrawal we delete the analytical cookies available to us and no longer load the counters. The statistics already collected remain depersonalised and are not linked to you.

5. Browser signals

If the browser transmits Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, we treat this as a refusal of analytics and do not show the banner: the counters are not loaded.

6. Analytics without identification

Google Analytics is configured with anonymize_ip. In Yandex.Metrica, Webvisor is disabled — we do not record mouse movements, keyboard input or page content. User identifiers for advertising are not transferred.

7. Changes

The list of cookies is updated together with changes on the site; the revision date is indicated at the top of the page.
