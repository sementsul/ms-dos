4. Refunds in Russia

The consumer has the right to withdraw from the contract at any time, reimbursing the costs actually incurred (Article 32 of the Law "On the Protection of Consumer Rights"). For a digital product that has already been downloaded, a refund is considered individually, taking into account that the result has been provided.

  1. Write to {{EMAIL}} from the address used for the purchase.
  2. State the date, amount, payment identifier and reason.
  3. We will reply within 10 working days.
  4. If the decision is positive, the refund is made by the same method as the payment, within up to 10 working days (the bank may credit the money later).

5. Refunds in the European Union

A 14-day right of withdrawal applies (Directive 2011/83/EU). For digital content supplied immediately, the right ends after the buyer's explicit consent and confirmation of the loss of the right of withdrawal — such confirmation is given by the download button after payment.

If the file has not been downloaded, withdrawal is accepted within 14 days without giving reasons. Regardless of withdrawal, the conformity guarantees under Directive (EU) 2019/770 are preserved for 2 years: if the program does not work as described, we will bring it into conformity, replace it or refund the money.

6. Refunds in the USA

Refunds are voluntary: write within 30 days of the purchase. We will refund the payment if the issue could not be resolved by support. Rights under the law of the state of residence are not limited by this.

7. When a refund is not made

  • the licence was revoked for breach of section 5 of the terms (cracking, circumventing the trial period, distributing the build);
  • the request was received after the file had been downloaded and used, and the complaint concerns properties that are expressly described on the website (for example, the absence of UEFI support or of a built-in MS-DOS);
  • the purchase was made not from the licensor but from a third party — in that case the refund is governed by that seller's rules.

A refusal is always reasoned with a reference to a specific term, so that you can appeal the decision.
