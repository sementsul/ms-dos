5. Cookies and analytics

Until “Accept analytics” is clicked, the site does not set analytical cookies and does not load Yandex.Metrica and Google Analytics scripts. A detailed list of the files, their lifetime and purpose is in the section “Cookies”. We have no necessary cookies without which the site does not work: the consent choice is stored in the browser's localStorage.

6. Retention periods

  • the record of consent to cookies — 12 months, then we ask again;
  • analytical reports and cookies: _ga — up to 2 years, _gid — 24 hours, _ym_d and _ym_uid — 1 year, _ym_isad — 1 day;
  • support correspondence — 3 years from the last message;
  • information about payment and the license — the period established by tax law (at least 4 years);
  • technical logs of the hosting — under GitHub Pages rules, usually no more than 30 days.

After the period expires, the data is deleted or depersonalised.

8. Cross-border transfer

GitHub Pages and Google Analytics are located outside the Russian Federation, and using them involves cross-border transfer. We enable Google Analytics only after your consent; if you refuse, no data is transferred to the USA. Yandex.Metrica processes data on the territory of Russia.

For users from the EU: transfer to the USA relies on the recipients' certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and on standard contractual clauses. You can request information about the safeguards by writing to sementsul.maksim@yandex.ru.

9. Your rights

Under 152-FZ and the GDPR you have the right to:

  • obtain information about the processing of your data and a copy of it;
  • request that the data be rectified, blocked or erased;
  • withdraw your consent and object to processing;
  • restrict processing and request data portability;
  • not be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing;
  • lodge a complaint with Roskomnadzor (for Russia) or with the supervisory authority of your place of residence (for the EU).

Write to sementsul.maksim@yandex.ru — we reply within 10 working days under 152-FZ and within 30 days under the GDPR. To protect against third-party requests, we may ask you to confirm that the request is yours.

10. Rights of US residents

If you live in California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah or another state with a privacy law, you have the right to know which categories of data we collect, to request their deletion or correction, to obtain a copy, and not to be discriminated against for exercising your rights.

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for contextual advertising. The “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” link is cookie settings: refusing analytics is equivalent to refusing “sale” and “sharing” in the terms of the CPRA. We honour the Global Privacy Control signal automatically.

Requests are accepted at sementsul.maksim@yandex.ru. We reply within 45 days; the period may be extended by a further 45 days, of which we will notify you separately.

11. Security

The site operates over HTTPS. Access to mail and to the admin panels is restricted and protected by a password and two-factor authentication. We do not store payment data. In the event of an incident affecting your rights, we will notify the affected users and, where required, the regulator.

12. Children

The site and the program are not addressed to children under 13 years of age (in the EU — under 16 years of age), and we do not knowingly collect their data. If such data has come to us, write to us — we will delete it.

13. Changes to the policy

The current revision is always published on this page with a date. In the event of material changes we will update the date and, if the changes concern consent, ask you to confirm your choice again.
