Buy MBFUTool 2.0 and get the files

Version dated 18 November 2026.

The full version is sold on Boosty: one payment, no subscription, no recurring charges. Here is what you buy, how payment works, what arrives afterwards and what to do if something goes wrong.

What you get

  • MBFUTool 2.0.0-trial build for Windows 10/11 x64 — the same functionality as the Trial, without the time limit.
  • A personal licence for one computer. A second computer needs a separate licence purchased separately.
  • Critical 2.x updates free of charge, in the same private Boosty post.
  • Installation support for 30 days after purchase at sementsul.maksim@yandex.ru.
  • A self-employment tax receipt issued in the “Moy nalog” app.

The package does not include MS-DOS, Norton Commander or any other third-party software: you supply the system image yourself. Lawful ways to get one are on the “Where to get MS-DOS” page.

Price

The current price is shown on the project page on Boosty. The site does not duplicate it: if it changes, you see it where you pay. The charge is one-off.

How the purchase works

  1. Open the project page on Boosty.
  2. Place a one-time purchase and pay with any method Boosty offers.
  3. After payment the private post with the files unlocks — usually at once, at most within 24 hours.
  4. Download the archive (or the installer), the README and the licence.
  5. Verify the SHA-256 against the value published on this site — see “How to verify the build”.
  6. Run the program as administrator and accept the licence on first start.

Payment and paperwork

Boosty is the only way to buy. Invoices, bank transfers, EDI and corporate contracts are not offered. If a company pays, paying by company card works: the tax receipt goes to the buyer and the licence still covers one computer.

Card details and wallet credentials are entered on Boosty's side; ms-dos.su never receives or stores them.

If access did not unlock

Write to sementsul.maksim@yandex.ru with the date and amount of the payment and the account used. We will check and send the file manually. Replies within two business days.

Refunds

Terms and deadlines are on the “Payment and refunds” page. In short: if the file has not been downloaded, a refusal is accepted within 14 days; once downloaded, the statutory rules for software apply.

Questions

How is the full version different from the Trial?

Only by the time limit. The Trial has all four operations but stops starting after 30 days. The full version has no limit. See “Trial and the full version”.

Do I need an activation key?

No. The Trial and the full version are separate builds: after purchase you download a build with no counter, nothing to enter.

Can a company buy it?

Yes, by company card on Boosty. Invoices, bank transfers and EDI are not offered, and there are no multi-seat licences: each licence covers one computer.

Does the licence expire?

No, it is a one-time purchase. Version 2.x stays yours, and critical 2.x updates are free.
