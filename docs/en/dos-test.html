Where to get an image

  • Use your own licensed copy of MS-DOS: a boot floppy image can be made from the original.

Questions and answers

What is the difference between 5.00 and 6.22 in the emulator?
Type VER: you will see 5.00 or 6.22. 6.22 includes SCANDISK, DriveSpace and MemMaker; 5.00 is lighter and closer to the 286-era original.

The emulator says “No bootable device” — what is wrong?
The image is not bootable or is not a floppy image (an ISO, for example). You need a bootable 1.44 MB IMG.
